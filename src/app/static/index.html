How SUMARO Works

Traditional navigation apps stop updating or guess wildly when GPS signals are lost in tunnels or urban canyons. SUMARO bridges the blackout gap using four coordinated steps:

1

Satellite Fixes

Under open sky, GPS fixes calibrate motion sensors and establish an accurate velocity and direction baseline.

2

Motion Sensing

Smartphone accelerometers and gyroscopes sample vehicle motion 50 times per second, detecting every turn and bump.

3

Intelligent Estimator

Our mathematical filtering engine strips away sensor noise and gravity leakage to accurately integrate physical displacement.

4

Seamless Recovery

When you exit the tunnel, GPS seamlessly blends back in, correcting any accumulated sensor drift without jumping.
